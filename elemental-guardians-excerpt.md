# 📐 Project Spec: "Elemental Guardians" Excerpt
* **Intent:** This sample showcases contemporary conversational rhythms and character voice development during ordinary, low-stakes everyday encounters within an urban fantasy setting.
* **The Motifs:** Parallel universes, corporate design systems (Dairy Cream-On-The-Go vs. Woodrow Industries), and hidden folklore layers.

## 🍦 Excerpt: 

### Donna's Application at Dairy Cream-On-The-Go...

Donna drove to Phillips Dairy Cream-on-the-Go, because she wanted to get that application out of the way first, she wanted to devote all her time and attention to the second job. The lady at the desk, an older lady, had a grandmotherly aura about her. The office she worked in smelled like fresh baked apple pies. 

"Hello dear, may I help you this morning?" the grandmotherly woman asked. 

"Yes ma'am, I am here about the job, that was in the paper, for the whip cream assembler," replied Donna. 

"Oh yes, I am sorry dear that position was filled about 20 minutes ago. Would you like to fill out an application anyway? You never know when something unexpected happens here. We need new personnel all the time." 

"Yes ma'am, while I am here I might as well, so the time won't be for nothing." 

"You've got a good head on your shoulders; I'll make sure to put some good notes on your application, and keep it at the top of the file." 

"Thanks," Donna said, blushing slightly, "you seem to be very good at your job; you made me feel very welcome here." 

It was the receptionist's turn to blush. She waited while Donna filled out the application. Donna handed the application to the lady, and went back to her car. As Donna left, she knew that this would be a good place to work for, eventually, but she hoped she could get the second job and work on the float. She felt that would be a much better use of her talents and time.
